About

Infrastructure engineer who has built from the ground up

I started in the field — pulling fiber, terminating cables, configuring switches, and rolling out hardware to stations across the rail network. That foundation turned into a career operating and securing infrastructure that thousands of people depend on every day.

Over the last 9 years at PT Kereta Commuter Indonesia, I have moved through roles covering hardware & networking, IT operations, infrastructure & data center, and security operations. I have designed and maintained multi-site WANs, deployed SD-WAN and firewalls, built VMware and Nutanix hyperconverged clusters, managed active-active failover between DC and DRC, and responded to security incidents using enterprise EDR, NAC, SIEM, and threat intelligence platforms.

I am comfortable working across the full stack: from physical cabling and CCTV/NVR deployments to cloud-adjacent virtualization, PKI, SSL, and vulnerability assessment. I communicate best in writing, so I prefer chat or email for clear, documented collaboration.
